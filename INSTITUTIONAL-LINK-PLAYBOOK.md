# Institutional Backlink Operating Playbook

Updated: 2026-09-26

## Goal
Earn editorial links from universities, education providers, research organizations, and public-interest information pages by offering useful housing-subscription data and tools.

## Primary assets
- https://apttosell.com/cheongyak-score-calculator/
- https://apttosell.com/cheongyak-score-data/
- https://apttosell.com/housing-subscription-data/
- https://apttosell.com/private-housing-deposit-data/

## Qualification
A target is worth pursuing when at least two are true:
1. It already links to private real-estate tools or data services.
2. It teaches proptech, real-estate data, housing subscription, or housing information.
3. It maintains a related-sites/resource page.
4. It has a real editor-facing contact path such as department office, course proposal, Q&A, or program manager.

## Preferred acquisition paths
1. Existing related-sites/resource page
2. Broken/outdated link replacement
3. Educational supplementary material
4. Course/resource proposal
5. Research or journalism citation

## Exclude
- User-generated boards
- Profile/directory links
- Paid guest posts
- Link exchanges
- Automated backlink packages
- Mass outreach

## Broken-link workflow
1. Find an institutional page containing an old real-estate service URL.
2. Verify that the old service is inaccessible, discontinued, or no longer relevant.
3. Match the replacement to the original intent.
4. Suggest one canonical AptToSell page, not the whole site.
5. Re-check the institutional page after the next crawl/indexing cycle.

## Success criteria
A successful link should be:
- editorially placed by the institution;
- contextually relevant;
- on a page that is indexable;
- pointing to a canonical resource page;
- acquired without payment, exchange, or mass submission.
