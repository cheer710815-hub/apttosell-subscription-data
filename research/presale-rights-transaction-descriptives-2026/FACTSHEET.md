# N01 citation factsheet — South Korea apartment presale-rights transactions (2026)

**Source:** AptToSell (2026), *South Korea Apartment Presale Rights Transactions: Regional and Size-Based Descriptive Statistics, September–October 2026*, v2.0, Zenodo. DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23432291

**Research article:** https://apttosell.com/presale-rights-transactions-region-area-2026/

## Key findings (within this selected extract, not nationwide estimates)

- **980** person-to-person, broker-mediated presale-rights transaction records, with September–October 2026 contract months, drawn from an October 2026 MOLIT data download. Excluded records with cancellation indicators at extraction.
- **689 of 980 (70.3%)** belonged to the 60–under-85 m² exclusive-area band.
- **Gyeonggi-do: 238 (24.3%)**; Chungcheongnam-do: 108 (11.0%); source region label `전남광주통합특별시`: 98 (10.0%); Busan: 87 (8.9%); Gyeongsangnam-do: 83 (8.5%). These five source-label groups total 614 (62.7%). Retain the source's merged region label verbatim; do not infer its comparability to older administrative boundaries.

September 2026: **590**; October 2026: **390**. October is incomplete at the October extraction date. **Do not calculate or describe a monthly market growth/decline rate from these counts.**

## Essential restrictions on interpretation

The extract is neither a census nor a probability sample of all national presale-rights transactions. Late reporting, corrections and cancellations can change the underlying data. Reported transaction amounts are **not** presale premiums, seller profits, or investment returns; these would require original project price schedules and a reconciled amount definition. Data provenance: MOLIT Real Estate Transaction Price Disclosure System. AptToSell aggregate documentation is CC BY 4.0 subject to original-source conditions.
