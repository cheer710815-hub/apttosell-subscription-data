# New independent study — September 2026 housing supply indicator comparability

Research ID: `ARHRN-SUPPLY-STAGE-COMPARABILITY-20261103`
Initiated: 2026-11-03
Site: AptToSell
Status: **SOURCE SCOPING ONLY; no independent research dataset or DOI**.

## Why this is distinct from existing work
Existing ARHRN work tracks apartment pre-announcements vs official notices, expected occupancy, and completion seasonality. This pilot instead tests **indicator definition and denominator consistency** across permits, construction starts, sales/marketing supply, and completions in the September 2026 official monthly housing statistics. It must not duplicate existing cohorts or interpret same-month stage counts as flows for the same projects.

## Source
MOLIT September 2026 housing statistics published 2026-10-30, 21-page source mirrored by KDI:
https://eiec.kdi.re.kr/policy/materialView.do?num=288812

The source reports:
- September permits: capital region 17,620; rest of country 8,318.
- September starts: capital region 9,308; rest 6,738.
- September sales/marketing supply: capital region 6,508; rest 13,243.
- September completions: capital region 9,730; rest 8,719.
- Uncompleted unsold homes national total 65,941; completed unsold 27,549, as at September month end.

**Critical definition issue:** these indicators refer to different activity stages, may cover different housing universes (e.g., permit/start/completion for all housing, sale for multi-unit housing) and are **NOT** a single month's conversion cohort. Ratios such as 9308/17620 are not construction-start conversion probabilities. Similarly, completed unsold is a subset of unsold inventory: the categories must not be summarily added.

## Key research question
What can and cannot be compared across same-month region-level supply-stage statistics? Produce a crosswalk of coverage, unit, reference period, source denominator, release revision and project alignment rules, then only calculate meaningful, explicitly conditioned comparisons.
