## Indicator crosswalk

| Indicator | Universe as identified by September release | Time | What it means | Cross-indicator ratio |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Permits | All housing | September 2026 activity | Permits issued/approved | Not a cohort denominator for September starts |
| Starts | All housing | September 2026 activity | Construction starts | Not a same-month conversion rate from permits |
| Sales / presale | Apartment/multi-unit housing ('공동주택') | September 2026 activity | Sales supply activity | Population differs from all-housing permit/start/completion |
| Completions | All housing | September 2026 activity | Completed homes | Different project/vintage cohort from September starts |
| Unsold homes | Unsold homes | Stock at end-September 2026 | Unsold inventory | Stock, not September flow |
| Unsold after completion | Completed unsold subset | Stock at end-September 2026 | Completed unsold inventory | Subset of unsold inventory; never add the two stocks |

## Important historical-revision issue
MOLIT metadata records statistical-approval revision dated 2025-09-25 for permits/starts/completions and a retrospective restatement of 2014–2024 statistics as announced 2025-09-30. It records permits/starts on final approval dates, completions on first approval dates (first temporary/partial/full use approval point), and explicitly warns some duplicate permits may survive harmonization. Monthly estimates are preliminary and next September's year-end revision is final.

Comparing historical releases directly, without preserving version, adjustment and revision date, is risky. This motivates a *data-version dictionary*, not a claim of conversion-rate research.

## Value and no-duplication decision
The stage-universe mismatch is already explicitly documented in the official release. A 1-month comparison/article merely restating it has **limited originality or external-citation value** and does not justify its own Zenodo DOI or prolonged statistical research. This project is CLOSED at source-crosswalk stage, unless a distinct, verified machine-readable revision-history dataset can later be justified without duplicating existing ARHRN housing supply studies.
