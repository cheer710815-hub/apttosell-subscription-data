**Description (EN):**
An open research and educational dataset that structures South Korea's 2026 private-housing subscription score system (84-point framework) and region/area-specific required deposit amounts. It prioritizes official notices and housing-subscription sources and provides machine-readable CSV/JSON files, methodology, validation principles, and reuse information. Users should verify the latest official recruitment notice before making an actual housing application.
