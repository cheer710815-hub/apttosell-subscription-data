# Methodology

## 1. Study population

The source population consists of apartment presale announcements issued from January through September 2026.

The analysis focuses on projects containing an approximately 84㎡ exclusive-use-area type.

## 2. Presale price

For each project, the analysis uses the maximum presale amount for the 84㎡-class unit type.

Clearly identical multi-block or multi-complex projects were combined to avoid duplicate weighting. For grouped projects, the median of the component projects' maximum 84㎡ presale prices is used as the research-level value.

## 3. Existing-apartment comparison group

Existing apartment transactions are selected using:

- same legal dong (법정동),
- transaction date within the six months before the presale announcement,
- exclusive-use area from 82㎡ to 86㎡,
- cancelled transactions excluded where cancellation information is available.

The median transaction price is used.

## 4. Age-based comparison groups

Three comparison groups are calculated where sufficient observations exist:

1. All apartment ages
2. Apartments completed within 20 years of the announcement year
3. Apartments completed within 10 years of the announcement year

## 5. Minimum sample rules

Research statistics are calculated only when at least five comparable transactions are available for the relevant comparison group.

The conservative publication-ready sample requires:

- at least 20 transactions in the all-age group; and
- at least 10 transactions in the 10-year group.

For the publication-ready sample, four atypical supply cases were additionally excluded so that the published headline statistics better represent ordinary new-apartment presale comparisons. The excluded cases were: one reserved-unit case, one post-conversion vacant-unit case, one cancelled-member unit case, and one follow-up public-sale case. These observations remain in the broader deduplicated research sample.

## 6. Presale price gap

`(presale price - median comparable transaction price) / median comparable transaction price × 100`

Comparison-group sensitivity is expressed in percentage points:

`all-age gap - 10-year gap`

## 7. Deduplication

Clearly identical multi-block or multi-complex developments sharing the same project identity, timing, location, and comparison transaction pool were combined into one research observation.

Projects merely sharing the same legal dong or announcement date were not automatically merged.

## 8. Limitations

This is descriptive analysis. It does not control for exact distance, floor, orientation, view, brand, project scale, redevelopment premium, option packages, school district, station accessibility, or all other product-quality differences.

The dataset should not be used as a direct measure of price fairness or investment value.

## 9. Recommended use

Appropriate:
- journalism on "nearby market price" comparison methods,
- housing-market research,
- classroom exercises,
- sensitivity analysis.

Not appropriate:
- labeling a project overvalued or undervalued solely from the gap value,
- investment recommendations,
- causal claims.
