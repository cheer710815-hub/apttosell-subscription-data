# AptToSell Supply Size × Follow-Up Supply within 90 Days 2026 — v0.1

## Overview

This derived dataset examines whether the observed rate of a first follow-up supply notice within 90 days differs by initial project supply size.

The cohort is restricted to projects with at least 90 days of observation time after the initial recruitment announcement.

## Cohort

- Eligible projects with 90-day observation: **140**
- Follow-up within 60 days: **73 (52.1%)**
- Follow-up within 90 days: **83 (59.3%)**

## Main descriptive result

Observed 90-day follow-up rates by supply-size band:

- **<300 households:** 58.3% (35 / 60)
- **300–499 households:** 45.5% (15 / 33)
- **500–999 households:** 65.5% (19 / 29)
- **1,000+ households:** 77.8% (14 / 18)

The pattern is not monotonic at the lower bands, but the 1,000+ household group had the highest observed 90-day follow-up rate.

## Important interpretation

This is an observational descriptive analysis.

It does **not** establish that larger projects cause more unsold units, contract failures, or cancellations. A follow-up supply notice can reflect several mechanisms, and project size is correlated with many other factors such as location, pricing, unit mix, market timing and demand.

The outcome is **follow-up supply observed within 90 days**, not an unsold-rate or contract-failure-rate measure.
