# Methodology — Supply Size × Follow-Up Supply within 90 Days 2026 v0.1

## Research question

Do observed first follow-up supply rates within a fixed 90-day observation window differ across project supply-size bands?

## Eligibility

Projects were included only when at least 90 calendar days had elapsed between the initial recruitment announcement and the observation cutoff.

This reduces right-censoring that would occur if very recent projects were compared with older projects.

## Exposure

Initial total supply households were grouped into:

- under 300 households
- 300–499 households
- 500–999 households
- 1,000 households or more

## Outcome

`followup_within_90d` indicates whether a matched first follow-up supply notice was observed within 90 calendar days of the initial recruitment announcement.

The dataset also reports the corresponding 60-day rate.

## Results

- <300: 35 / 60 = 58.3%
- 300–499: 15 / 33 = 45.5%
- 500–999: 19 / 29 = 65.5%
- 1,000+: 14 / 18 = 77.8%
- overall: 83 / 140 = 59.3%

## Limitations

- The analysis is descriptive and unadjusted.
- Supply size may be associated with region, pricing, competition, brand, unit mix and other variables.
- Follow-up supply is not equivalent to unsold rate, non-contract rate, contract cancellation rate or winner abandonment.
- The 1,000+ group contains only 18 projects, so its percentage should be interpreted with its smaller denominator in mind.
