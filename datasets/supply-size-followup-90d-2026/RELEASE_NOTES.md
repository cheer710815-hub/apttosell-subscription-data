# Release Notes

## v0.1 — 2026-10-08

Initial release of the supply-size × 90-day follow-up descriptive dataset.

### Coverage
- 140 projects with at least 90 days of observation
- 83 projects with a matched follow-up supply notice within 90 days

### 90-day observed rates
- <300 households: 58.3%
- 300–499 households: 45.5%
- 500–999 households: 65.5%
- 1,000+ households: 77.8%

### Interpretation limit
This is not an unsold-rate, cancellation-rate or contract-failure-rate dataset and does not establish causality.
