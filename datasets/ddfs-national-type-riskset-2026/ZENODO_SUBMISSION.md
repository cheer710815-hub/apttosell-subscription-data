## Description
Type-level research dataset for 184 South Korean apartment housing types across 52 projects with a verified first follow-up supply event in 2026. The risk set includes only official housing types whose initial general-supply first-priority aggregate competition was at least 6.0x. The dataset links initial competition depth, type supply share, pricing and financing covariates to whether the identical official housing type reappeared in the first follow-up supply.

Among 184 eligible housing types, 78 reappeared in the first follow-up supply and 106 did not, for a type-level reappearance rate of 42.39%. In the primary project-clustered logistic model, competition per doubling was associated with lower reappearance odds (OR 0.538, 95% CI 0.360–0.804, p=0.0025), while type supply share per doubling was associated with higher reappearance odds (OR 1.249, 95% CI 1.038–1.503, p=0.0184).

This dataset measures housing-type reappearance in the first follow-up supply. It does not measure cancellation rate, contract failure rate, winner abandonment, or individual loan availability. The study is observational and does not establish causality.
