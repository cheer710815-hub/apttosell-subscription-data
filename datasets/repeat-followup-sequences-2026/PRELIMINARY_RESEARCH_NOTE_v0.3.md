# Repeated Follow-up Supply Notices in South Korean Apartment Presales (2026) — Preliminary Research Note v0.3

**Status:** Preliminary descriptive analysis; official-notice validation incomplete. **Not a DOI release.**

**Source:** AptToSell internal *Competition vs Followup 2026 v1.0*, Followup_Events sheet. This is a derivative study of the existing follow-up notice corpus, not a newly collected nationwide census.

## Descriptive findings (2026-10-13)
- 103 matched projects with 141 follow-up notice events in the source workbook.
- 22 projects with at least two observed follow-up notices; 60 events within those projects.
- Distribution: 12 projects with two notices, 6 with three, 2 with four, and 2 with five.
- 38 consecutive event pairs; median announcement-date gap 38.5 days.
- One event pair has a zero-day gap; the temporal order of these notices is not established.
- For 38 adjacent pairs, notice-level offered unit counts decreased in 32, stayed unchanged in 3, and increased in 3.

## Validation and limitations
- CSV internal checks: 0 detected date-gap/count/URL-format inconsistencies.
- Official source notice checks recorded for **4 of 60** event rows; **56 are not independently checked**. All numbers above are provisional.
- Notices can re-offer the same units. Summing supply counts or interpreting a reduction as contract conversion, cancellation rate, or absorption is invalid.
- The project matching and source population inherit the limitations of the existing AptToSell source workbook.
- Same-day events may be parallel announcements, not successive rounds.
- This question concerns *repeated notice sequences* and differs from the already registered DDFS type-level first-followup reappearance study. However, the underlying data overlap; do not issue a new DOI without central review of derivative status.
