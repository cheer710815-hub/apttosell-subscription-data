# Repeated Follow-up Supply Notices in South Korean Apartment Presales (2026) — Preliminary Research Note v0.3 (corrected 2026-10-15)

**Status:** Descriptive research with official-notice major-information verification completed; dwelling-level audit has a documented source limitation. **Not a DOI release.**

**Source:** AptToSell internal *Competition vs Followup 2026 v1.0*, `Followup_Events` sheet. This is a derivative sample, not a nationwide census.

## Descriptive findings
- Source workbook: **103 matched projects**, **141 follow-up notice events**.
- Repeat-sequence subset: **22 projects**, **60 notice events**.
- Sequence distribution: 12 projects with two notices, 6 with three, 2 with four, 2 with five.
- **59 distinct project-date observations** and **37 positive consecutive announcement-date gaps**. Median positive gap **40 days**, observed range **6–130 days**.
- One same-day pair (Yongin Platform City Raon Private Ardier, 2026-04-22) comprises parallel notices, not an established chronological transition. Do not include a zero-day interval in the positive-gap median.
- Notice-level supply counts and repeated individual dwelling IDs are distinct measures. Equal notice counts can represent different units, and smaller notice counts do not prove completed contracts.

## Official-document validation (2026-10-15)
- Official ApplyHome major-information pages checked for **all 60/60 notice events**: date and advertised supply count matched the research manifest.
- Official PDF dwelling-level comparisons performed for **21/22 projects**. The remaining project, Uijeongbu Station Central I-Park (ATS-2026-035), has **no unit identifiers in its first notice**, so first-to-second notice reappearance cannot be established from that source.
- The original source's UNSOLD/OPTIONAL labels must not be presented as independently verified legal notice types across all 60 events unless the PDF type audit is completed.
- 'Newly appearing' means absent from the chosen prior announcement, **not** newly constructed or a proven contract cancellation.
- Unit-level evidence examples: Bucheon Station Epit Urbanic has two eight-unit rounds with only five identical dwellings; Acro Riversky repeats all three same dwellings; Incheon City Hall Forena The Sharp's later rounds introduce different dwellings.

## Limits and release control
- This is a selected cohort drawn from an existing workbook, not a representative national incidence estimate.
- Official announcements do not directly measure executed contracts, cancellation rates, or sell-through.
- Source PDFs, unit-extraction evidence, code, and data dictionary should be included in a versioned deposit; reproducibility and license review remain publication gates.
- Existing related Zenodo registry: DOI **10.5281/zenodo.23176906**. The present repeat-sequence study overlaps its source corpus but asks a different research question.
- **No new Zenodo DOI or external institutional deposit has been issued for RFSEQ.** The central research ledger is the source of truth for submission status.
