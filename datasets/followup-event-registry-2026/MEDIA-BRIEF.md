# Media Brief — 2026 Apartment Follow-Up Supply Registry

AptToSell linked the first observed follow-up supply event for **103 apartment projects** in South Korea's 2026 subscription market to a stable project ID and official notice identifier.

## Headline findings

- First follow-up events linked: **103**
- Numeric official follow-up notice IDs recovered: **103 / 103**
- Canonical official source links: **103 / 103**
- Direct-primary official-artifact verification: **13**
- Additional corroborated rows with official ApplyHome links: **90**

## Why this matters

The registry turns a project-level “follow-up supply observed” flag into a reusable event-level reference layer. Researchers, journalists and analysts can join a project to the first later supply notice without relying on project-name matching alone.

## What this does not mean

This dataset does not estimate contract cancellation, non-contract or unsold rates. A later follow-up notice may result from multiple causes and must not be interpreted as a direct measure of failed contracts.
