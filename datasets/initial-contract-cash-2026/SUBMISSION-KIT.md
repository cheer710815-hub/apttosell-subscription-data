## Short description

A South Korea apartment presale dataset based on January-September 2026 initial housing recruitment notices. It standardizes contract deposits, interim-payment structures, financing support, balance structure, and a pre-move-in funding ratio for cross-project comparison.

## Verification scope

- Validation universe: **196 projects**
- Payment structure + financing conditions verified: **82 projects**
- Pre-move-in funding ratio calculable: **81 projects**
- Conservative public subset: **41 projects**

## Standard metric

**Pre-move-in funding ratio = total contract deposit rate + explicitly self-paid interim-payment rate**

The metric does not estimate personal loan eligibility or actual bank approval. Later residual-unit or first-come promotional conditions are kept separate from the initial recruitment conditions.
