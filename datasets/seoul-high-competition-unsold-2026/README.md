# Seoul High-Competition Unsold Cases 2026

AptToSell case dataset for seven Seoul apartment projects that recorded strong first-priority subscription competition and later had a first no-rank re-supply.

## Summary
- Projects: 7
- Initial general-sale units: 2,747
- First no-rank re-supply units: 153
- Average re-supply units per project: 21.9
- Median re-supply units: 15
- Weighted re-supply share: 5.57%
- Mean initial first-priority competition rate: 25.57:1

## Important
No-rank re-supply does not always mean contract cancellation by buyers. Causes are classified separately as contract give-up, ineligibility, residual units after regular and reserve contracts, or unknown when the detailed reason was not publicly disclosed.

Contract-cancellation re-supply caused by illegal conduct is treated as a separate category and is not mixed with ordinary unsold/residual cases.
