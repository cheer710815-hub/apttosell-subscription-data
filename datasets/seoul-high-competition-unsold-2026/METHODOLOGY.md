# Methodology

## Research question
Why did no-rank re-supply occur even after high subscription competition?

## Sample
Seven major 2026 Seoul private apartment projects with high initial first-priority competition and a verified first no-rank re-supply.

## Main measures
- Initial first-priority average competition rate
- Initial total general-sale units
- First no-rank re-supply units
- First no-rank re-supply share = re-supply units / initial general-sale units x 100
- First no-rank competition rate

## Cause categories
- CONTRACT_GIVEUP: contract give-up explicitly confirmed
- INELIGIBILITY: ineligibility explicitly confirmed
- RESERVE_EXHAUSTED_RESIDUAL: units remaining after regular and reserve contract procedures
- RESIDUAL_UNKNOWN: residual units confirmed but detailed cause not publicly separated
- CONTRACT_CANCELLATION_ILLEGAL: re-supply after contract cancellation related to illegal conduct; treated separately from ordinary no-rank residual supply

## Evidence grades
- A: official notice, developer statement, or major report with a specific cause
- B: re-supply and unit count verified, but exact cause composition not publicly disclosed

## Limitations
The sample is not a representative probability sample of all Seoul apartment launches.
No-rank re-supply units are not equivalent to an unsold rate or contract give-up rate.
Buyer motives such as premium expectations cannot be directly observed from subscription data.
