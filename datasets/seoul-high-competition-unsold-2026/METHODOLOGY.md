# Methodology

The dataset compares initial first-priority competition with the first later no-rank supply for seven Seoul projects.

first_unsold_share_pct = first_unsold_units / initial_general_sale_total * 100

Cause labels are assigned only when supported by a notice, project source, or specific reporting. Unknown causes remain unclassified.

Do not interpret no-rank units as a cancellation rate or unsold-rate statistic.
