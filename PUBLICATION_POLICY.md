# Research publication and citation policy (audit draft)

Effective as a protective interim policy on 2026-11-24; human approval required before changing official publication status.

1. **Do not submit** new files to Figshare or Zenodo, automate access to suspended accounts, create evasion accounts, or generate replacement DOIs.
2. **One canonical scholarly deposit per distinct research release**, only if the selected provider permits it. Additional destinations should generally link to the existing DOI rather than silently re-minting the same content. Follow each provider's actual rules; no general assertion that every cross-deposit is forbidden.
3. Before any future external release, complete: official-source provenance and re-use rights; prior work overlap matrix; materially new research question/analysis; reproducibility and data quality; accurate author/AI disclosure; research purpose and provider fit; verified research-specific citation; explicit human sign-off.
4. **Distinguish** publication from pilot, draft, GitHub documentation, version, mirror and derivative. Derivative products of the same 196-project source must cite that cohort and state their own added work. Do not count them as independent observations.
5. Keep historical DOI identifiers and frozen records for traceability. Corrections should be logged and tied to a version or erratum; never imply an original immutable archive has been edited.
6. Avoid mass-generated repositories, keyword-stuffed description, bulk promotional cross-linking or automated notification patterns. Research evidence and reusability take priority over search authority.
7. On any violation concern: stop external submissions, preserve evidence, request provider clarification, and issue a truthful correction where necessary.
8. This README/policy change does not authorize publication to any third-party repository or a claim of academic peer review.

See RESEARCH_INVENTORY.md and DOI_REGISTRY.md for outstanding checks.

A source being publicly accessible does not by itself establish permission to redistribute or relicense its contents. Record the original source, acquisition date, applicable reuse terms and third-party rights. A license for independently created material applies only to material for which the relevant rights are held. Preserve required attribution and do not impose additional restrictions on material already licensed under CC BY 4.0. Where source rights remain uncertain, prefer links and independently derived summaries over redistributing original files.

Count research families, release versions, external deposits, metadata mirrors and independent third-party citations separately. A change of platform, language or title alone does not establish a new study. Research purpose, distinct contribution, reproducibility, accurate disclosure of AI assistance and human review, and provider eligibility must be documented before any future deposit.
