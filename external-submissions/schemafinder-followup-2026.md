## Description
Project-level dataset for 196 South Korean apartment presale projects announced from January through September 2026. It links first-priority subscription competition rates, presale-price information, and later residual, unsold, or optional-supply announcements. Competition-rate coverage is available for 193 projects. Follow-up supply is an observed subsequent supply announcement and is not equivalent to a contract-failure or non-contract rate.
