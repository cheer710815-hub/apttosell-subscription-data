# Korea Apartment Subscription Follow-up Supply 2026

## About Dataset

This dataset connects 2026 South Korean apartment presale announcements with first-priority subscription competition rates, presale-price bands, and later residual/unsold or optional-supply announcements.

### Coverage
- Initial recruitment announcements: January–September 2026
- Final cohort: 196 projects
- Competition-rate coverage: 193 projects
- Observation cutoff: 2026-10-04

### Headline result
Among 44 projects with a first-priority competition rate of at least 10:1, 19 later had a matched follow-up-supply announcement. The simple observed rate was 43.2%. Among 36 projects with at least 60 days of observation time, 13 had follow-up supply within 60 days, or 36.1%.

### Important interpretation note
Follow-up supply is not a contract failure rate. It only indicates that an official later residual/unsold or optional-supply announcement was matched to the initial project.

### Source
Derived from Korea Real Estate Board ApplyHome public API data.
