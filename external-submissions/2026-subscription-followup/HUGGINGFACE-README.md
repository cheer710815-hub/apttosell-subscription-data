# Korea Apartment Subscription Competition and Follow-up Supply 2026

AptToSell이 2026년 1~9월 최초 모집공고 아파트를 기준으로 청약 경쟁률, 분양가, 무순위·잔여세대·임의공급 후속공급을 연결한 공개 분석 데이터입니다.

## Key findings

- Final cohort: 196 projects
- Competition-rate coverage: 193 projects
- First-priority competition >=10:1: 44 projects
- Follow-up supply observed among >=10:1: 19 projects
- Simple observed rate: 43.2%
- 60-day eligible cohort: 36 projects
- Follow-up within 60 days: 13 projects
- 60-day rate: 36.1%

## Important note

“Follow-up supply” is not the same as a contract failure rate or non-contract rate. It means that a later official residual/unsold or optional-supply announcement was identified and matched to the initial project.
