# ARHRN Research Value & Earned Citation Gate (2026-11-04)

**User-directed mandatory policy. No deposits for the sake of obtaining DOIs, directory listings or SEO backlinks.**

## STOP by default
A finished report is *not* automatically eligible for deposition. Pause all new external deposits until this gate is passed. Do not create repeated versions, duplicate research objects, or multiple repository mirrors to multiply links. Zenodo account moderation has an unknown cause; do not describe unverified cause as fact.

## Mandatory six-gate decision
1. **Real question and audience:** Name the real-world decision the work helps and at least one credible prospective external audience (housing researcher, data journalist, policy analyst, nonprofit, specialist publisher). Generic 'SEO readers' does not qualify.
2. **Original empirical contribution:** Go beyond a transcription, summary or reformatting of available official figures; document a reproducible new comparison, measurement, verification method, or reusable structured dataset. Distinguish novel contribution from already-published ARHRN work.
3. **Evidence:** Verify source provenance, terms/license, completeness, code, raw inputs or legally redistributable derivatives, checksums, denominators, interpretation limits, factual accuracy, and author accountability. Unverifiable claims fail.
4. **Independent reuse:** Supply a method, code/data dictionary, machine-readable results, and a usable conclusion relevant to external researchers. A PDF created merely to include a homepage URL fails.
5. **Earned citation pathway:** Identify specific plausible editors/research groups who could cite the output, what exact table/finding they would use, and a natural canonical source reference. A repository link alone does not count as an earned backlink. Avoid keyword-rich promotional anchors, forced links, or link exchange.
6. **Measurable result:** Before submission set a 30/60/90-day review plan: live editorial citations, relevant referring domains, qualified referral visits, downloads/reuse when measurable. If evidence of meaningful reuse remains absent, stop routine serial deposits and improve the research and outreach instead.

## Decision states
- **PUBLISH**: all six gates supported and the target repository accepts the research object; exactly one suitable DOI archive if needed, with canonical source and related identifiers.
- **IMPROVE**: real utility but originality, reproducibility or use-case insufficient; research remains unpublished.
- **SITE-ONLY**: helpful explanatory article but not an independent research artefact; publish only on the first-party site if editorial quality passes.
- **STOP**: duplicative, promotional, unverifiable, or no independently useful result; no DOI or repository deposit.

## Zenodo moderation prevention
Zenodo states its automated and manual moderation can mistakenly flag accounts. It excludes promotional submissions and AI-generated content without verifiable human research basis. Require accurate authorship, descriptive metadata, related identifiers and minimal non-promotional linking. Await actual support decision before attributing any specific cause.
