# Media Brief — High Competition Did Not Always End Follow-Up Supply

## One-line finding

Among 140 apartment projects with a full 90-day observation window, **83 projects (59.3%) had a first observed follow-up supply event within 90 days**, while the result varied sharply by initial first-priority competition band.

## Quote-ready numbers

- Fixed 90-day observation cohort: **140 projects**
- First follow-up event within 60 days: **73 projects, 52.1%**
- First follow-up event within 90 days: **83 projects, 59.3%**
- Median time to first follow-up among the 83 90-day hits: **42 days**
- No project in the fixed 90-day cohort had its first observed follow-up event within 30 days.
- 10–30:1 competition band: **15 of 18 projects, 83.3%** had a first follow-up event within 90 days.
- 1–5:1 competition band: **27 of 31 projects, 87.1%** within 90 days.
- 30:1 or higher: **1 of 15 projects, 6.7%** within 90 days.

## Band table

| First-priority competition band | 90-day eligible projects | Within 60 days | Within 90 days |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Below 1:1 | 65 | 31 (47.7%) | 32 (49.2%) |
| 1–5:1 | 31 | 24 (77.4%) | 27 (87.1%) |
| 5–10:1 | 11 | 6 (54.5%) | 8 (72.7%) |
| 10–30:1 | 18 | 11 (61.1%) | 15 (83.3%) |
| 30:1 or higher | 15 | 1 (6.7%) | 1 (6.7%) |
| Total | 140 | 73 (52.1%) | 83 (59.3%) |

## Why this is different from ordinary competition-rate articles

Most coverage reports the competition rate at the time of subscription.

This analysis connects the initial first-priority competition rate to the **first later official follow-up supply notice** and uses a fixed observation cohort so that 30/60/90-day figures are comparable.

## Important caveat for publication

Do not describe the 59.3% figure as an unsold rate, cancellation rate, or contract-failure rate.

A follow-up event only means that a later official supply event was observed.

The result is descriptive and does not prove that the initial competition rate caused the later follow-up event.
