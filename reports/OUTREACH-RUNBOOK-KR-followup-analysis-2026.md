# Korean Outreach Runbook — Follow-Up Supply Analysis 2026

## Goal

Turn AptToSell's public housing-subscription data into actual third-party citations, research use, and natural backlinks.

## Core asset

Analysis: first-priority competition-rate band vs. first observed follow-up supply within 30/60/90 days.

Fixed comparison cohort: 140 projects with a full 90-day observation window as of 2026-10-04.

Primary public files:

- `reports/apttosell-followup-by-competition-band-90d-cohort-2026.csv`
- `reports/METHODOLOGY-followup-by-competition-band-90d-cohort-2026.md`
- `reports/MEDIA-BRIEF-KO-followup-by-competition-band-90d-cohort-2026.md`

## Anti-spam rule

- Never bulk-send the same body.
- Keep the personalized opening sentence.
- Do not send follow-up mail if there is no response for less than 5 business days.
- One follow-up maximum unless the recipient replies.
- Do not contact multiple people in the same organization on the same day when the message is substantially identical.
- For CERIK, Huh Yoon-kyung and Kim Sung-hwan should be separated rather than sent simultaneously.

## Success classification

- SENT = outbound completed
- REPLIED = any human response received
- REQUESTED_DATA = recipient asked for files or clarification
- CITED = published citation or attributed use
- LINKED = external page links to AptToSell/GitHub/DOI
- NO_RESPONSE = no reply after follow-up window
- DECLINED = explicit decline
- BOUNCED = delivery failed

Do not classify a sent email as a result.

## Follow-up window

Check responses after 2 business days.

If no response, wait until at least 5 business days after the original send before considering one short follow-up.

## Citation request

Do not ask for a backlink directly.

Use:

> 자료를 사용하실 경우 출처는 AptToSell로 표기해주시면 됩니다.

A natural source link is preferred but should not be demanded.

## Caveat that must stay in every version

Follow-up supply is not the same as unsold rate, contract-failure rate, cancellation rate, or sales completion rate.

The analysis is descriptive and does not establish causality.
